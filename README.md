# Ayisha F R — Multidisciplinary Design Studio Portfolio

An expressive, tactile, and modern portfolio website showcasing multidisciplinary design practice across **Furniture**, **Spatial / Interior**, **Industrial Product**, and **Graphic Identities**.

---

## 🎨 Visual Identity & Inspiration
The portfolio embodies an editorial, tactile aesthetic:
* **Organic Retro Pop**: Bold ink line-work, retro sticker stamps, running marquee ticker, and warm chromatic gradient ribbons.
* **Editorial Typography**: Pairing avant-garde display titles (`Syne`) and classical editorial serifs (`Playfair Display`) with clean, legible sans-serif body typography (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).
* **Curated Layout**: Clean visual breathing room, spacious project cards, and interactive case study dialogues.

---

## ✦ Key Sections

1. **Top 3 Highlighted Signature Works**:
   * **01. L’Onde Sculptural Lounge** (*Furniture Design*) — Solid bent ashwood and raw bouclé wool with zero-gravity ergonomic contours.
   * **02. Atelier Solstice Gallery & Cafe** (*Interior / Spatial Design*) — 280 m² hospitality space featuring lime plaster colonnades and custom terrazzo islands.
   * **03. Kōra Acoustic Diffuser** (*Product & Graphics*) — Slip-cast terracotta and brass acoustic vessel paired with foil-stamped origami unboxing.
2. **Multidisciplinary Works Archive**:
   * Filterable gallery covering **Furniture**, **Interior**, **Product**, and **Graphics**.
3. **Four Pillars of Practice**:
   * Breakdown of design methodologies across all four disciplines.
4. **Designer Narrative & Philosophy**:
   * Studio story, milestones, and design ethos: *"Design is the tactile dialogue between human emotion and physical matter."*
5. **Interactive Studio Inquiries**:
   * Commission inquiry form with interactive validation and feedback confirmation.
6. **Dual Art Palette Modes**:
   * **Editorial Cream** (default warm paper canvas)
   * **Midnight Studio** (moody velvet dark theme)
