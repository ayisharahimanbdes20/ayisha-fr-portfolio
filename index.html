About the Designer

"Design is the tactile dialogue between human emotion and physical matter."

With a multidisciplinary foundation across industrial product design, interior scenography, and visual communication, I approach every brief not from a single medium, but as an interconnected ecosystem.

Whether shaping a bentwood armchair, engineering the mood of a 300-seat hospitality space, or curating the tactile grain of an unboxing experience, my practice champions restrained simplicity, honest textures, and playful wit.
